# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individual Codex users and engineering teams reviewing their own local session history. They work at a desktop workstation and need to answer what they used, when they used it, and how the API-equivalent estimate was calculated without uploading their conversation data.

## Product Purpose

Codex Token Dashboard turns local JSONL session usage into an inspectable, timezone-correct dashboard. It helps users reconcile token usage, identify models and workspaces, compare periods, and keep lightweight local budgets while preserving the source data and user control.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, and private. The product should feel like a reliable engineering instrument: concise labels, visible accounting context, and honest unavailable or partial states.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a consumer SaaS analytics portal, a promotional pricing calculator, a chat transcript viewer, or an auto-updater that acts without consent. Avoid decorative charts, hidden data transformations, vague success messages, and settings that imply a remote action can succeed when it cannot.

## Design Principles

1. Local evidence first: derive every figure from the latest successful in-memory snapshot and make scan state visible.
2. Accounting over spectacle: show range, pricing coverage, quality, and comparison context beside the numbers that depend on them.
3. Familiar desktop controls: standard tables, native form controls, disclosures, keyboard access, and explicit disabled states beat invented interaction patterns.
4. User retains control: refresh, export, update checks, and local configuration are explicit actions; the application never uploads, changes, or installs data by itself.
5. Honest recovery: retain the last successful snapshot during a refresh, distinguish stale results from current results, and explain unavailable functions in place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard-only use, visible focus, semantic controls and tables, accessible canvas alternatives, polite status announcements, and complete Traditional Chinese and English UI text. Colour never carries status without text, and precision controls retain readable labels at the supported desktop width.
